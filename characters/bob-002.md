- **Output:** 1024x1024 JPEG, saved to `characters/bob.jpg`

## Note on `--size`

`--size=300x1200` is ignored here — flux always returns a fixed 1024x1024
image regardless of the requested size. The flag only has an effect with
`--type=png`, which uses `@cf/stabilityai/stable-diffusion-xl-base-1.0` and
does honor `width`/`height`. To actually get a tall, full-body composition
that isn't fighting a square crop, use:

```
/generate-image Create a complete anime character: head, torso, arms, legs, and shoes (this is not a portrait, but a complete person): a bald 57-year-old with glasses and a crazy white beard, wearing a black hoodie and jeans. Nothing more; keep the background empty. --type=png --size=300x1200 --filename=characters/bob.png
```
